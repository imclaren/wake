What it does

wake sits in the menu bar. Turn its switch on and the Mac does not go to sleep when you close the lid. It keeps running whatever it was doing, such as a download, a build or a backup, until you turn the switch off.

How it works

  1. Turning it on asks for an administrator's password, because the setting that stops a closing lid putting the Mac to sleep is one only an administrator may change.
  2. Sleep is disabled only while the lid is open. The moment the lid closes, sleep is enabled again, and the Mac stays awake, because it is closing the lid that puts a Mac to sleep.
  3. Turn it off while the lid is closed and the Mac goes to sleep at once. Quitting the app does not put the Mac to sleep.

A MacBook that is awake inside a bag can get hot. Be careful when you pack one away with wake turned on.

Privacy

wake keeps nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The only connection it makes is to this site, to check for a new version.
