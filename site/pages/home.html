Stay awake. Even with the lid closed.

wake is a Mac menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake for as long as you choose. Keep it awake only while the lid is open, or keep a MacBook awake even after you close the lid, so that a download, a build or a backup runs until you turn it off.

{{if .Released}}Download for Mac{{end}} How it works

How it works

A switch in the menu bar, and a choice of how the Mac stays awake and for how long.

Mind the heat

A MacBook that is awake inside a bag can get hot. Be careful when you pack one away with wake keeping it awake.

Or with Homebrew

If you use Homebrew, it installs the same signed and notarised app into Applications.

brew install --cask imclaren/tap/wake

wake then updates itself, as it does when it is downloaded, so brew upgrade leaves it to do so. To remove it, quit wake and run the command below. Adding --zap removes its settings as well.

brew uninstall --cask wake

If wake was used with the lid closed, its rule for sudo stays until it is removed, which Homebrew cannot do for you.

sudo rm /private/etc/sudoers.d/wake-$(id -u)
